About the band

The Peanut Gallery is a collaborative music project bringing together different artists, personalities, and influences under one name. Created in collaboration with Bubbles, the project is built around a simple idea: music gets more interesting when everyone brings something different to it. Each collaboration gives The Peanut Gallery its own sound, shaped by the people involved rather than a single fixed style.
